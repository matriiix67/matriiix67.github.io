Restore purchases

Open the Me tab, tap Upgrade on the subscription card, then tap Restore Purchases.

iCloud sync

Make sure your device is signed in to iCloud and your plan includes sync (Pro), then turn on iCloud Sync in Me → Sync.

Look up, translate and read aloud

In the reader, tap a word to open the dictionary or double-tap a sentence to open system translation. Tap the speaker icon on the lookup card to hear the pronunciation in your device’s system voice.

Language

The app is available in English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese and follows your device language. To change the language for PageLeaf, open its app settings on your iPhone and tap Language.

Dictionaries

The built-in offline dictionaries are English–Chinese. If you’d prefer English definitions, use the System Dictionary tab in the lookup pop-up, which uses the dictionaries installed on your iPhone.
