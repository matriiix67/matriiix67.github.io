Little Artbook Support

Contact

For help or feedback, email matriiix67@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version and steps to reproduce the issue. Check artwork and screenshots for personal information before sharing them.

Purchases and restoration

Apple handles Premium purchases. Use Restore Purchases in the app’s Premium screen to restore eligible purchases for your current Apple Account. Manage subscriptions in your device’s Apple Account settings.

iCloud sync

Check that your device is signed in to iCloud and that iCloud access is enabled for the app. Sync timing depends on network and iCloud availability.
