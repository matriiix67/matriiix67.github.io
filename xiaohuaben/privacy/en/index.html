Little Artbook Privacy Policy

Effective date

November 21, 2026

Your artwork and data

The drawings you save and the original photos kept when you import them; each drawing’s date, title, note and voice recording; and the artist names, birthdays and avatars you set are stored in this app’s storage on this device. Little Artbook needs no account, has no servers of its own, and contains no ads, analytics, or other third-party components. The developer cannot see your drawings, photos, children’s names, or how you use the app.

iCloud sync

If you have Premium and the device is signed in to iCloud, this content is also saved to the private database of your own iCloud account so it can sync across your devices. That data is kept by Apple under your iCloud account; only you can access it, and the developer cannot read it. To stop syncing, turn off iCloud access for this app in the Settings app.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to photograph and scan artwork. When you import a photo, the app receives the item you select through the system photo picker. The app requests permission to add photos when you save artwork to your photo library.

Microphone

Used only when you record a voice note for a drawing. Recordings are stored with your drawings and are never sent to us or any third party.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple’s App Store handles Premium purchases and subscriptions. The developer does not receive your payment card details. The app receives purchase status from Apple to provide Premium features.

Sharing, exporting and deletion

You choose when and where to share or export artwork using the system share sheet. You can delete drawings or artbooks; when iCloud sync is enabled, deletions may sync to other devices. Removing the app deletes its locally stored app data.

Children’s information

This app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
