Little Artbook Privacy Policy

Effective date

November 21, 2026

Your artwork and data

The drawings you save, the original photos kept when you import them, pre-brightened photo backups kept so Paper Brightening remains reversible, each drawing’s date, title, note and voice recording, and the artist names, birthdays and avatars you set are stored in this app’s storage on this device. To power the “Today’s Drawing” Home Screen and StandBy widget, the app also caches downsampled artwork thumbnails and basic display metadata (title, date, and artist name) locally in its on-device App Group shared container; the widget makes no network requests. Little Artbook needs no account, has no servers of its own, and contains no ads, analytics, or other third-party components. The developer cannot see your drawings, photos, children’s names, or how you use the app.

iCloud sync

If you have Premium and the device is signed in to iCloud, this content is also saved to the private database of your own iCloud account so it can sync across your devices. That data is kept by Apple under your iCloud account; only you can access it, and the developer cannot read it. To stop syncing, turn off iCloud access for this app in the Settings app.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to photograph and scan artwork, with all image processing performed on-device. When you import one or more photos, the app receives only the items you choose through the system photo picker (without access to the rest of your library) and reads their EXIF capture dates locally on your device to record accurate drawing dates. The app requests add-only permission only when you save artwork to your photo library.

Microphone

Used only when you record a voice note for a drawing. Recordings are stored with your drawings and are never sent to us or any third party.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple’s App Store handles Premium purchases and subscriptions. The developer does not receive your payment card details. The app receives purchase status from Apple to provide Premium features.

Sharing, exporting and deletion

Your content leaves the app only when you actively choose to share a drawing (as an original image or Framed Card), export a PDF album, or export all drawings, and its destination is chosen solely by you in the system share sheet. Depending on your selections, Framed Cards and PDF albums may include the drawing title, artist name, age, date, and note. You can delete individual drawings, an artist’s artbook, or all drawings; when iCloud sync is enabled, deletions sync to your other devices. Removing the app deletes its locally stored app and widget cache data.

Children’s information

This app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
