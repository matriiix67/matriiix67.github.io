Little Sketchbook Privacy Policy

Effective date

November 15, 2026

Your artwork and data

Little Sketchbook stores artwork you choose, saved dates, and the young artists’ names you enter in its local database. The developer does not operate a server that collects this content.

iCloud sync

When your device is signed in to iCloud and the app has iCloud access, artwork and sketchbook information may sync between your devices through your private iCloud database. Apple processes iCloud data under its terms. You can manage the app’s iCloud access in system settings.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to photograph and scan artwork. When you import a photo, the app receives the item you select through the system photo picker. The app requests permission to add photos when you save artwork to your photo library.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple’s App Store handles Premium purchases and subscriptions. The developer does not receive your payment card details. The app receives purchase status from Apple to provide Premium features.

Sharing, exporting and deletion

You choose when and where to share or export artwork using the system share sheet. You can delete drawings or sketchbooks; when iCloud sync is enabled, deletions may sync to other devices. Removing the app deletes its locally stored app data.

Children’s information

Little Sketchbook helps families preserve artwork. Young artists’ names and artwork are stored on the device and may sync according to your iCloud settings. Parents or guardians should manage content relating to children.
