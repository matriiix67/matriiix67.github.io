PageLeaf Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

PageLeaf (朝读夕拾) lets you read EPUB books, look up words in dictionaries, track your reading progress and review vocabulary. This policy explains what data the app processes, why, where it is stored and how you can control it.

PageLeaf is developed and operated by Huang Jianli (黄建莉), an individual developer, who is the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

When you first launch the app, we show you a summary of this policy and ask for your consent. If the policy changes materially, we will ask again.

Data the app processes

  • EPUB files you import, book information and local file paths.
  • Reading progress, reading time, bookmarks, notes, and vocabulary study and review records.
  • App settings such as theme, font, lookup method and reminder switches.
  • Purchase status and subscription expiration dates provided by the App Store.
  • Text you select when you use a dictionary, translate a sentence or read aloud.
  • Your email address, issue description and any attachments you provide when you contact support.

On-device storage

Books, reading data and most settings are stored on your device by default. Deleting the content or uninstalling the app removes the related data from the device.

Any service credentials from an earlier version are kept in this device’s system Keychain and may remain after uninstalling the app. Before uninstalling, you can go to Me → Privacy & Data and use the available controls to revoke past permissions or clear credentials.

iCloud sync

Pro users can turn on iCloud sync under Me → Sync. It stores book information, reading data and some app settings in the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account so your devices stay in sync. Turning on automatic book uploads also syncs EPUB files. Retained service preferences from earlier versions may sync as part of app settings. Credentials are stored only on the device. Turning off iCloud sync stops further uploads. You can delete the app’s iCloud data using Apple’s iCloud storage settings.

Dictionaries, translation and read-aloud

The built-in offline dictionaries look up words on your device. The system dictionary, translation and read-aloud use Apple’s system features and process selected text according to your device settings. If both languages are downloaded, translation is processed on your device. Otherwise, Apple may process the sentence you selected. See Apple’s Translation & Privacy notice.

Where data is stored and international transfers

The developer does not operate a server that receives app data. If you contact support, the developer receives the email and any attachments you send. Apple provides iCloud, App Store purchases and system features and may process data for these services outside your country or region. See the Apple Privacy Policy. PageLeaf uses the purchase status and subscription expiration dates Apple provides to verify access.

How data is used and shared

The app processes data as needed to provide the reading, lookup, translation, read-aloud, review, sync and purchase features you request. The developer uses the support email you send to respond to your request. The app contains no advertising SDKs, does not track you across apps, and does not sell or share personal data for advertising.

Retention and deletion

  • On-device data is kept until you delete the content or uninstall the app.
  • iCloud data is kept until you delete it in the app or with Apple’s iCloud storage management.
  • Past service permissions are retained until you revoke them or uninstall the app. Use the available controls under Me → Privacy & Data to clear old credentials.
  • Privacy and support emails are kept until the issue is resolved and for as long as necessary to meet legal obligations.

Privacy choices

You can turn off iCloud sync under Me → Sync and manage your content in Library, the reader, bookmarks, notes and Study. To clean up data on your device, manage any past permissions or credentials shown under Me → Privacy & Data, then uninstall the app. You can manage iCloud data separately.

Your rights

Depending on the data protection laws that apply where you live (such as the GDPR in the EU and the UK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. You can view your reading data, export notes and delete content in the app. For help with information you shared with the developer, use the contact details below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not designed specifically for children. Children under 13 (or a higher age set by local law) should use the app only with the consent and guidance of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data without consent, please contact us.
